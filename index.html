Your impact matters

Six Ways to Cut Your Carbon Footprint

Make practical changes in your daily routine. Start with one action, build a habit, and inspire others to join you.

🚲

Travel Smarter

Walk, cycle, use public transport, or share rides when possible. Choose efficient or electric vehicles when you need to drive.

#LowCarbonTravel
⚡

Save Clean Energy

Switch off unused devices, improve home insulation, choose efficient appliances, and explore renewable electricity options.

#EnergyEfficiency
🥬

Eat More Sustainably

Enjoy more plant-rich meals, buy seasonal produce, plan portions, and reduce food waste wherever you can.

#SustainableFood
♻️

Use Less, Reuse More

Repair what you own, choose reusable products, avoid unnecessary packaging, and recycle materials accepted locally.

#CircularLiving
🌳

Protect Nature

Support habitat restoration, protect existing forests, plant suitable native species, and care for local green spaces.

#NaturePositive
📣

Spread the Word

Share reliable climate information, support effective climate policies, and encourage workplaces and communities to act.

#ClimateAction
